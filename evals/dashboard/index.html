Movable evals depend on things that change without altering runtime behaviour or a public contract (documentation, generated docs and types, annotations, additive token routing, ARIA, key handlers, state styles) and are worked in the refine loop. Structural evals depend on the DOM contract, the cascade or the composition model; moving them is a breaking change, documented with its behavioural impact (B-4 to B-8) and waiting on an owner decision, so they are expected to stay flat here. Roadmap evals move with new components or features, which are neither refactors nor breaking changes.
